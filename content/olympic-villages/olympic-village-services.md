# Olympic Village Services

Olympic Villages provide a wide range of services so athletes and team officials can manage daily needs without regularly leaving the village. In addition to housing and dining, these services can include medical care, fitness facilities, transportation, recreation, laundry, banking, and other support. Bringing many of these resources together in one controlled area allows athletes to focus on training and competition while organizers coordinate services for thousands of residents throughout the Games.

## Training Facilities in the Olympic Village

Training and fitness facilities give athletes places to maintain their conditioning and prepare for competition while staying in the village. These facilities can include gyms, strength and conditioning equipment, stretching areas, and other general training spaces. Sport-specific training usually takes place at separate practice or competition venues because many sports require specialized facilities. Village fitness areas instead provide convenient spaces for athletes to continue regular workouts, warm up, recover, and maintain their training routines around competition schedules.

### Medical Facilities in the Olympic Village

Medical facilities provide athletes and team officials with access to healthcare during the Games. Village medical centers can offer general medical treatment, emergency care, physical therapy, imaging, pharmacy services, dental care, and other forms of support. Athletes may also have access to medical personnel from their own national teams. Centralized medical services allow many health concerns and injuries to be evaluated within the village, while patients who require more specialized treatment can be transferred to hospitals or other medical facilities outside the Olympic Village.
