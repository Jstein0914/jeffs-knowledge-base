title: About Me
---

I have been interested in sports for most of my life. Growing up, I played baseball, football, soccer, and tennis, and I also enjoyed waterskiing. I have always been competitive and enjoyed the challenge of playing different sports. As I have gotten older, I am not able to participate in sports like I used to, but my interest in them has never gone away. Today, I enjoy watching and following almost any type of sporting event. The Olympics have always interested me because they bring together so many different sports, athletes, and countries in one event. That lifelong interest in sports is why I chose the Olympics as the focus of my knowledge base.
