# Winter Olympic Sports

The Winter Games feature a wide range of sports that take place on snow and ice and test abilities such as speed, endurance, precision, balance, and teamwork. Some sports have been part of the Winter Olympic program since the first Games in Chamonix in 1924, while others have been added as winter sports have developed. Bobsleigh, figure skating, ice hockey, ski jumping, and speed skating were among the competitions held at Chamonix. More recently, sports such as freestyle skiing and snowboarding became part of the Winter Games, while ski mountaineering made its Olympic debut at Milano Cortina 2026.

## Sports and Events of the Winter Games

Winter Olympic sports and disciplines can include multiple events that use different competition formats. Alpine skiing, for example, includes downhill, super-G, giant slalom, and slalom, while snowboarding includes halfpipe, slopestyle, big air, snowboard cross, and parallel giant slalom. Ski mountaineering at Milano Cortina 2026 included men's and women's sprint events and a mixed relay. These differences allow the Winter Olympic program to include competitions that vary significantly in format and the skills required of the athletes.

### Major Categories of Winter Olympic Sports

Winter Olympic sports can be grouped in different ways based on the type of activity, playing environment, and how athletes compete. Some sports can belong to more than one group. Ice hockey and curling are team sports played on ice, while figure skating and speed skating also take place on ice but use very different forms of competition. Some of these groups include:

- **[[winter-team-sports|Winter team sports]]:** ice hockey and curling
- **[[winter-individual-sports|Winter individual Sports]]:** alpine skiing, biathlon, figure skating, speed skating, ski jumping, luge, skeleton, snowboarding, and ski mountaineering
- **Skiing sports:** alpine skiing, cross-country skiing, freestyle skiing, ski jumping, Nordic combined, and ski mountaineering
- **Skating sports:** figure skating, speed skating, and short track speed skating
- **Sliding sports:** bobsleigh, luge, and skeleton
