# Team Sports

The first Winter Games in Chamonix in 1924 already included team competition, with ice hockey and curling both part of the program. Ice hockey had previously appeared at the 1920 Olympics before becoming part of the Winter Games, while curling was contested at Chamonix. Although the Winter Olympics include fewer team sports than the Summer Games, team competition is not limited to sports built entirely around teams. Sports that are primarily based on individual competition also include team events, such as relays and other team-based race formats.

## Team Competition in the Winter Olympics

Olympic team competitions use different formats depending on the sport and event. Ice hockey begins with preliminary-round play before teams advance to elimination rounds leading to the medal games. Curling uses a round-robin format in which teams compete against one another before the highest-ranked teams advance to the semifinals and medal games. Other Winter Olympic sports also include team events, such as team pursuit in speed skating and relays in short track speed skating, cross-country skiing, and biathlon.

### Major Olympic Team Sports

The Winter Games include team sports that differ in the number of competitors, playing surface, equipment, and basic style of play. They also include team events within sports that are primarily individual competitions. Major examples include:

- **Ice hockey:** six players per team are normally on the ice at a time, including a goaltender, with teams using sticks to move the puck and score in the opponent’s goal.
- **Curling:** teams take turns sliding stones toward a target called the house, with points awarded based on which team has stones closest to the center after each end.
- **Speed skating:** includes team pursuit, where two teams race over a set distance to determine which team advances or wins.
- **Short track speed skating:** includes relay events in which skaters compete as teams around a short oval track.
- **Cross-country skiing:** includes relay and team sprint events in addition to individual races.
- **Biathlon:** includes relay events that combine cross-country skiing and rifle shooting.
